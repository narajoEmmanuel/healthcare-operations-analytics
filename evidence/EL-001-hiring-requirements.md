## Objective

Identify the capabilities this portfolio must visibly demonstrate for target analyst roles and convert them into project design priorities.

## What Emmanuel personally completed

Emmanuel defined the target roles, provided the earlier market-research synthesis, selected the employability focus, and specified the skills gap this project must address. Repository drafting and the current source re-check were performed with AI assistance and remain subject to Emmanuel's review.

## Technologies and sources used

Official employer career pages were reviewed through web search on September 4, 2026. The live sample included Optum/UnitedHealth Group and CVS Health postings. Git and Markdown were used to preserve the resulting research and evidence.

## Verified statements

The reviewed official postings explicitly referenced combinations of SQL, structured/relational databases, Power BI or comparable visualization, dashboards, modeling, data validation/quality, metrics or KPIs, requirements gathering, stakeholder collaboration, and communication of actionable insights. This supports prioritizing those capabilities in the portfolio.

No prevalence percentage, posting count, salary conclusion, or claim that every target role requires every listed tool was calculated.

## Validation procedure

1. Compared the supplied EL-001 synthesis with a current search of official employer career domains.
2. Inspected posting content returned from official career pages/search results.
3. Recorded only recurring themes supported by the inspected sample.
4. Marked career links as time-sensitive and separated critical MVP skills from optional tools.

## External source evidence

The authoritative URL register and access dates are in [`../research/references.md`](../research/references.md). Some postings were indexed at verification time but subsequently returned 404 when opened, a normal limitation of time-sensitive vacancy pages.

## Limitations

The current live sample is small, includes roles at different experience levels and geographies, and is directional rather than statistically representative. The broader synthesis is inherited from prior research supplied by Emmanuel and was not fully reconstructed during this task.

## What this evidence does not prove

It does not prove labor-market prevalence, employer endorsement, job readiness, mastery of any technology, or completion of the analytical platform.
