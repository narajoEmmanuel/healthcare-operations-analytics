## Established boundaries

- Descriptive analysis only unless a later requirement supports a different method.
- No patient-level conclusions, clinical decision support, causal claims, hospital adoption claims, or CMS endorsement.
- Supporting datasets will be added only when an analytical question requires them.
- The selected data represent Original Medicare fee-for-service inpatient activity, use aggregated records, and suppress some low-volume observations. Detailed source constraints are maintained in [`market_and_dataset_research.md`](market_and_dataset_research.md#key-limitations).

No business result is claimed until the problem and questions are approved and the data are acquired and validated.
